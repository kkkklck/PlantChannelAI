# Results interpretation draft: publication validation benchmark v1.0

## Proposed primary result

In the frozen 30-case benchmark, human consensus truth comprised 10 direct-conduction (D), 5 indirect ion-related (I), 12 alternative-mechanism (A), and 3 unresolved (U) cases. B5 recovered 0/10 D cases as DIRECT_PROMOTE and made 0/17 direct promotions among I/A cases. B5 returned NONDIRECT_WITHHOLD for 20/30 cases and ABSTAIN for 10/30 cases.

## What this result supports

B5 behaved conservatively with respect to direct promotion on this panel: no I/A case was promoted directly. The observed result is compatible with a mechanism-upgrade blocking role, in which available frozen historical evidence is insufficient to justify a direct-conduction claim.

## What this result does not support

The same benchmark provides no evidence that B5 can discover direct-conduction cases, because direct recovery was 0/10. The result must not be presented as evidence of biological validity for any individual B5 output, zero general error, superior overall performance, or a basis for adjusting B5 after unblinding.

## Reporting numbers

- Direct recovery: 0/10 (0.0%; exact 95% CI 0.0%–30.8%).
- Unsupported direct promotion among I/A: 0/17 (0.0%; exact 95% CI 0.0%–19.5%).
- Non-abstention coverage: 20/30 (66.7%; exact 95% CI 47.2%–82.7%).
- Abstention: 10/30 (33.3%; exact 95% CI 17.3%–52.8%).
- Correctly resolved coverage among D/I/A: 14/27 (51.9%; exact 95% CI 31.9%–71.3%).

## Recommended conclusion sentence

Under the prospectively frozen mapping and independent human truth set, B5 produced no observed unsupported direct promotions among I/A cases but did not recover any consensus D cases; accordingly, this benchmark supports B5 only as a conservative direct-claim withholding mechanism and does not support its use as a direct-conduction case-discovery tool.

## Governance boundary

Benchmark v1.0 is evaluated and closed to tuning. Any rule, mapping, model, or threshold change would require a new version and an untouched independent validation panel.
