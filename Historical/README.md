# Historical records

These are retained original records, not current execution instructions.

- `V1_RESULTS_INTERPRETATION_DRAFT_V1.0.md` is the historical interpretation draft for the closed 30-case V1 experiment: 10 D, 5 I, 12 A and 3 U reference cases; B5 direct promotion 0/10 D and 0/17 I/A; 20 withholding and 10 abstention outputs. Its recorded asset hashes refer to internal historical originals, not to this path-sanitized public package. A complete V1 input/output release is not included here.
- `V2.1_RECRUITMENT_DEVIATION.md` records the failure to obtain the planned 80 eligible cases: 71 title-level candidates, 30 exclusions and 41 potentially usable screened cases. V2.1 stopped because of the recruitment shortfall. The current 80-case analysis belongs to the separately registered mixed-source V3 study.
- `V3_PROTOCOL_FROZEN_original.md` and `V3_SEARCH_PLAN_FROZEN_original.md` retain the original V3 plans. Later procedural changes, actual initial extraction routes and the single-author reference review are described in the root README, `AI_USE.md`, S6 and S18. Planned personnel/routing fields in historical documents are not evidence of completed procedures.

V1 results were already known when V3 was designed. Forty-one V3 cases had been screened previously; 39 were newly recruited. No historical record has been rewritten to claim uninterrupted V2.1 execution or an entirely fresh random panel.
